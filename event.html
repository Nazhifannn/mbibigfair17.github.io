MBI BIG FAIR

MBI Big Fair (MBF) is an annual event hosted by MBI Amanatul Ummah located in Pacet, Mojokerto. For nearly 16 years, MBI Big Fair has consistently delivered a variety of competitions and spectacular events each year.
